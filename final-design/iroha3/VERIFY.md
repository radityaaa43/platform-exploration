## Conventions
- RED/GREEN: the "RED (before)" and "GREEN (after)" columns are the quoted lines qa must see. A row without a RED line is "not testable" with the reason.
- Fresh evidence: runtime verdicts use a tx made after the step's rollout (driver image, pod start). - Lock/cap: a flow is a live write. `flock /tmp/lensa-paladin.lock` around ONE detached run, demo amounts, serial, lead GO. Binding cap for the iroha3 suite (lead 2026-11-11): `IROHA3_TOPUP=1` only with a GO, at most 2 top-ups per flow and 6 per run, each exactly `topUpAmount()` (RD floor 3,000,000; DFABI floor 20); a third top-up for one flow aborts the run. Release the lock only by killing the holder qa started. STOP and report on `Driver APS gagal` / ENOENT / `connection closed before message completed`; no retry without a GO.
- BI-balance proof (flow steps S10, S13, S16, S18): `frontend/web/e2e/flows/bi-delta.ts` iroha3, per cell: rd-issue RD -1001 / DFABI 0; rd-transfer 0/0; dfabi-issue RD -1 / DFABI -3; dfabi-transfer RD -1 / DFABI 0; dvp 0/0; award RD +1,000,000 / DFABI -1. The fee cells (rd-issue RD, dfabi-issue RD, dfabi-transfer RD) are [re-derive after S10]: they hold only while BI is the sponsor (SPONSOR_REIMBURSEMENT 1 paid by the RD(leg 0) payer; with no RD leg a BI->BI 1 RD companion leg, C:63-65).
