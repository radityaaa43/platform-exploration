## Why every validator and self-submit cell is GAP (one root cause)
One cluster, one cluster-admin kubeconfig (alibaba.yaml, mode 664), one Lensa host that holds or can read every party's keys:
- Paladin: 4 HD seeds in Secrets node1..4.keys readable by one admin, disableSignRPC=false (paladin@v1.0.0 doc-site/docs/architecture/key_management.md; administration/configuration.md:344); one basic-auth secret for 4 ingresses; dev sample topology (concepts/cloud_native.md:18).
- Stealth: frontend/server/state/stealth.json holds banks A/B/C spend+view+identity keys and BI deployer key; Lensa signs and scans for all (stealth.ts:113,117,348-349). BLOCKER vs EIP-5564 per-recipient keys.
- Fabric-X: every CA, orderer, FSC key a Secret in ns fabricx; rdbi-driver-files holds every party's view-client key; shared ids/anon Org1MSP submitter (README deviation 2); 3-of-4 is our choice (rdbi/topology.go:84 vs committer docs/namespace-policy.md configurable). Upstream: parties independent admin units (fabric-x-orderer@dd296e3 docs/architecture.md §8).
- Iroha 3: every bundle sponsored by BI (aps.rs:207, pools.rs:273,346 `cfg.sponsor("bi")`); scenario Job and Lensa mount all four seeds; one operator generated all peer keys (network/generate.sh:2-4). Upstream: sponsor = any carrier signer (carrier.rs:215; spec :457-496), each validator its own key (spec :44-46).
